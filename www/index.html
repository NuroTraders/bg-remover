    How to use BG Remover

    1. Add Images: Click "Add images", drag & drop files, or press Ctrl+V to paste from your clipboard.
    2. Choose AI: The "Fast" model is built-in and instant. For perfect edges on hair and complex items, download the "High quality" model (requires WebGPU).
    3. Adjust: Use the fine-tune sliders if the AI missed a spot. You can also change the background color, blur it, or upload a new image.
    4. Brush: If the AI still missed something, use the Touch-up Brush to manually restore or erase parts.
    5. Save: Choose PNG for transparency, JPG for solid backgrounds, and click "Save". You can also copy it directly to your clipboard.

    About BG Remover

    Version 1.3.0

    A free, open-source background removal tool that runs 100% locally on your device.

    Privacy First: Your photos are never uploaded to a server. The AI runs directly inside your browser or desktop app using ONNX Runtime and WebGPU.
